Agentic OS (Personal)

Agentic OS is a private, self-hosted assistant dashboard that Yvinson Pierre runs on his own computer for personal and Eureka Fieldworks use. It is not offered to the public.

It connects to his own Google account, with his permission, to show his recent email and calendar events in one place and help him keep track of his work. No other users' data is accessed.
