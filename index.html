What we do

Eureka Fieldworks currently offers a fixed-scope service for commercial / institutional electrical subcontractors. We review customer-confirmed approved change orders against the matching pay applications / schedules of values / invoices to identify potential billing gaps (approved dollars that appear missing or only partial on billing records).

We do not provide:

  • legal advice or entitlement determinations
  • debt collection
  • negotiation with general contractors
  • guaranteed cash recovery
  • software seats or ongoing accounting/CPA services

Current paid offer

Approved Change-Order Revenue Recovery Sprint — fixed price $1,500 (USD), one-time.

For a frozen cohort (the buyer’s two most recently closed pay-app cycles, capped at the chronologically newest 25 approved change-order lines), we:

  • match customer-confirmed approved CO records to pay apps / SOV / invoices
  • deliver an evidentiary gap table (human-confirmed flags)
  • deliver a customer-executed checklist
  • help design a lightweight no-new-field-app reconciliation ritual the buyer runs

AI tools may help extract, normalize, and flag candidates. Final gap determinations require explicit per-line confirmation by the buyer’s controller / billing owner. Target delivery: gap report within 10 business days after receipt of a complete bound data pack and settled payment.

Purchasing is not open on this page. Payment and commercial activation are offered only when separately authorized for a qualifying engagement.

Guarantee / refund

If the artifact-matched gap dollars on a bound, complete pack are under $2,500, a refund is available within 7 days of gap-report delivery. The buyer may keep the report.

The guarantee is void if material Stage-1 / cohort lines are omitted from the Stage-2 pack without a documented exclusion and reason.

Cancellation

This is a one-time fixed-scope service, not a subscription. There is no recurring cancellation. After payment and before delivery, contact us to discuss canceling the engagement. After the gap report is delivered, refund eligibility follows the guarantee terms above.
