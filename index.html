Your website is often the first impression

Before calling, a homeowner may look your company up online. What they find — a slow site, a confusing site, or no site at all — can shape whether you get the call.

Hard to use on a phone

If a customer lands on your site from their phone and your number isn't one tap away, reaching you becomes work — and a customer who can't reach you easily may simply try the next company.

Services buried or missing

If visitors can't quickly see what you do and where you work, they may never learn you offer it — and may end up contacting a company that made those details easier to find.

Reviews nowhere to be seen

Your good reputation is your best salesperson — but only if visitors can see it. If your Google reviews are buried or missing, visitors may miss the strongest reason to choose you.
