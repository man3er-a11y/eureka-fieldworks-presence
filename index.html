What Eureka is

Eureka Fieldworks is a small business-creation layer. We find a specific, painful customer problem, run a bounded offer to learn whether people will pay and get value, and only then consider scaling.

Eureka is not a large venture studio, investment fund, or multi-brand portfolio company. It is operated by one founder building carefully, one venture at a time.

How we work

  • Start from a real customer problem, not a product idea in search of a buyer.
  • Validate with a focused, fixed-scope offer before adding complexity.
  • Keep only what customers pay for and get value from.
